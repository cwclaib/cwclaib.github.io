The History of Waldo

Waldo is a NCSU Pep Band trumpet section tradition dating back to circa 2011. Here is his history as given to me by his first owner, Michelle Parker-Van Dyke:

"Waldo came into my possession sometime in either fall of 2011 or early spring of 2012. The attached photo [below] is from the first game where we got Waldo. That was the first of the games where the student government would pass out large signs during the basketball games. They passed out the Waldo sign to the Pep Band. Waldo first went to David Higgins, pictured in the photo holding Waldo. There was a common procedure where you traded your student ID to the person who handed you the sign so that you'd return the sign at the end of the game. David Higgins had not done that but still didn't feel good about taking Waldo with him. I didn't want to leave Waldo behind so I took him home with me and then continued to tote him with me to all the basketball games until I graduated and Waldo got passed along to Will who was one of my roommates at the time."

After that, Waldo has attended nearly every basketball game with a member of the trumpet section until 2018-19. Will Blazer gave the cutout to his girlfriend, Lauren Kabel, who split responsibilities with Austin Kitts. The person Lauren had originally intended on giving the cutout to changed sections before the start of the 2018-19 season, and she did not know who a suitable replacement would be. After one season without Waldo, I reached out to Lauren, who was happy to have him back in action with the trumpets; after some minor repairs (applying packaging tape around the borders to prevent further deterioration, and foam board strips for durability), Waldo was back in action.

Unfortunately, Waldo was retired just a few years later, after the 2023-24 basketball season: while damage to the figure was a major contributor, a lack of interest among section members was also cited as a salient factor. A chance meeting with Tyler Mills at an IcePack game in February 2025 reunited the figure with me. Waldo now resides on the wall of my basement; his last public appearance was an IcePack hockey game on September 26th, 2025 against the University of Maryland at Wake Competition Center's Invisalign Arena. He managed to cheer the Pack on to one last victory: the Pack won 4-1.
